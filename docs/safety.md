# Safety Notes

This is a placeholder safety section for the build and demo process.

## Main Risks

- Pinch points in the blade linkage and servo arms
- Overcurrent or brownout if servos share insufficient power
- Mechanical failure of printed parts under load
- Unexpected servo movement during power-up or reset
- Sharp edges on printed or finished components

## Minimum Precautions

- Power the servos from a supply sized for stall current
- Keep hands clear during bench testing
- Test without wearing the assembly first
- Set conservative servo angle limits before full-range motion tests
- Inspect printed parts for cracks before use

## Demo Restrictions

- Do not wear or demonstrate around other people until motion is predictable
- Do not present the prop as safe for contact use
- Do not operate near eyes, face, or loose clothing
